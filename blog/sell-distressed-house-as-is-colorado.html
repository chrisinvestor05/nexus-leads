Why Selling a Distressed House As-Is for Cash Makes Sense in Colorado

Published November 2, 2026 · By Christian Younan

Many Colorado homeowners with neglected or damaged properties assume they must invest in repairs before selling. In reality, selling the house as-is for cash to a local buyer is often faster, cleaner and more certain.

The problem with listing a distressed house

Traditional listings on distressed homes frequently run into inspection issues, lender appraisal conditions and buyers who back out. Fire damage, water damage, foundation movement or an old roof can make a home hard to finance at all.

Repairs take time and capital

Contractors in the Denver metro are often booked weeks out, and permits for fire or structural repairs add more delay. Meanwhile you carry taxes, insurance and utilities on a vacant property.

How an as-is cash sale works

With a cash sale to Nexus, the offer is based on the property’s current condition. There are no showings, no repair requests and no financing contingency. You choose the closing date, and a local title company handles the closing.

Situations where it fits best

  • Inherited houses and probate estates
  • Fire damaged homes awaiting an insurance decision
  • Hoarder houses and full clean-outs
  • Tired landlords and tenant-occupied rentals
  • Homes that are simply decades out of date
