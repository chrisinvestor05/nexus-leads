Frequently asked questions

Do you really buy houses in any condition?

Yes. We buy houses in any condition across Colorado: fire damage, water damage, foundation issues, hoarder houses, boarded-up vacants and homes that simply need updating. You do not repair, clean or stage.

How fast can you close?

We close on the date you choose. Many sellers close within a couple of weeks; if you need more time to move, we can wait.

Are there commissions or fees?

No agent commissions and no fees from you. We cover normal closing costs.

How do you decide on an offer?

We look at the property’s current condition, the repairs it needs and recent sales nearby. Our offer is no-obligation, and we walk you through how we arrived at it.

Can I sell an inherited house in Denver that is still in probate?

Often, yes. We work with personal representatives and probate attorneys to time the sale correctly.

What areas do you serve?

Denver, Aurora, Lakewood, Arvada, Thornton, Westminster, Colorado Springs, Pueblo, Greeley, Fort Collins and the rest of Colorado.
